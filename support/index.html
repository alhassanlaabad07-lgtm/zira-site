Write to us at zira.app.contact@gmail.com. We answer every message by e-mail. ZIRA is made and run by Alhassan Laabad, an individual based in Morocco.

Your balance and your recovery code

ZIRA has no account and no password. Your token balance is linked to an anonymous identifier kept on your phone — in the web app, in your browser.

In Profile, your recovery code brings your balance back if you change phones, reinstall the app or your browser clears its data. Keep it somewhere safe, and share it with no one but us: anyone who has it can use your balance. If you have lost it, write to us; without it, recovering a balance may be impossible.

On iPhone

On iPhone, ZIRA opens in Safari, at tryzira.com/app. It sells nothing: as in the Google Play app, replies beyond the free messages come from short videos that you choose to watch, once the videos open there. If you watched a video and the reply did not open, write to us with your recovery code (in Profile): we will look into it.

If ZIRA becomes available on the App Store, tokens there will be bought through Apple. To ask for a refund, use Apple’s page: reportaproblem.apple.com. If you paid and the tokens did not reach your balance, write to us with your recovery code (in Profile) and the date of the purchase.

Your data

What ZIRA uses, keeps and shares is set out in the Privacy Policy.

To withdraw your consent, use Profile › Start over from scratch, or delete the app: your profile is erased from your phone, and nothing more is sent.

To have the data our server keeps deleted, write to us with your recovery code: it tells us which data is yours. Deleting it also deletes your token balance.

About Zira

Zira’s replies are written by an AI, a language model from OpenAI. They are for reflection and entertainment, and they are not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.

If one of her replies troubles you, write to us. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number now.
