Your balance and your recovery code

ZIRA has no account and no password. Your token balance is linked to an anonymous identifier kept on your phone.

In Profile, your recovery code brings your balance back if you change phones or reinstall the app. Keep it somewhere safe, and do not share it: anyone who has it can use your balance. If you have lost it, write to us.

Purchases on iPhone

On iPhone, tokens are bought through the App Store. To ask for a refund, use Apple’s page: reportaproblem.apple.com.

If you paid and the tokens did not reach your balance, write to us with the date of the purchase.

Your data

What ZIRA uses, keeps and shares is set out in the Privacy Policy. To have your data deleted, or to withdraw your consent, write to us with your recovery code from Profile: it tells us which data is yours.

About Zira

Zira’s replies are written by an AI, a language model from OpenAI. They are for reflection and entertainment, and they are not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.

If one of her replies troubles you, write to us. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number now.
