Before Galileo

Astronomy and astrology once went hand in hand.

The old model put the Earth at the centre, with the sky turning around it. The armillary sphere and the astrolabe were its instruments; the eye did the rest. The telescope has since taught us far more. It did not change what the naked eye sees. ZIRA starts from that view.
